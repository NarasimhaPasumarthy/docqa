# Acme Robotics Employee Handbook

Acme Robotics is a fictional company. This handbook is sample data for the DocQA demo.

## Working hours

Our core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone. Outside core hours, you can
arrange your schedule with your team. Meetings should be scheduled inside core hours
whenever possible.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. The other days are spent in the
office with your team. Working fully remote requires written approval from your VP and
is reviewed every 12 months.

## Paid time off (PTO)

Full-time employees receive 25 vacation days of paid time off per year. PTO accrues
monthly, at a little over 2 days per month. Request time off at least two weeks in
advance for absences longer than three days.

You can carry over up to 5 unused vacation days into the next year. Carried-over days
must be used by March 31, after which they expire.

## Sick leave

Employees get 10 paid sick days per year, separate from PTO. A doctor's note is required
after 3 consecutive sick days.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers
receive 6 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Leave can be taken any time within the
first 12 months after a birth or adoption.

## Travel and expenses

Submit expense reports through the expense portal within 30 days of the purchase,
with a receipt for anything over $25. Purchases over $500 need your manager's approval
before you buy.

The meal allowance when traveling for work is up to $75 per day. Flights longer than
6 hours may be booked in premium economy; all other flights are booked in economy.

## Learning and development

Every employee has an annual learning budget of $1,500 for courses, books and
conferences. Unused learning budget does not roll over.
