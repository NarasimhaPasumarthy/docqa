# Acme Robotics New Hire Onboarding Guide

Acme Robotics is a fictional company. This guide is sample data for the DocQA demo.

## Your first day

Arrive at 9:00 and pick up your laptop from the IT desk at the Building B front desk.
Bring a photo ID so we can print your badge. Your manager will meet you there and walk
you to your team.

## Your first week

Complete the security awareness training by the end of day 3. You will be paired with an
onboarding buddy, a teammate who answers day-to-day questions during your first month.
Schedule a 30-minute coffee with your buddy in your first week.

## Tools you'll use

We use Relay for chat, Tracker for tickets and project planning, and GitForge for source
code. Your accounts are created before your first day; sign in with your company email
and set up multi-factor authentication when prompted.

## 30/60/90-day plan

In your first week, agree on a 30/60/90-day plan with your manager. Your first formal
check-in happens at day 30, followed by check-ins at day 60 and day 90.

## Payroll

Payday is the last business day of each month. Your first paycheck may be prorated
depending on your start date.

## Getting help

The IT helpdesk is at extension 4357 (HELP) or through the helpdesk portal, staffed
08:00 to 18:00 on business days.
