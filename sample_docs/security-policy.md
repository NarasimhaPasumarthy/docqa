# Acme Robotics Information Security Policy

Acme Robotics is a fictional company. This policy is sample data for the DocQA demo.

## Passwords and authentication

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long; that is the minimum password length for
every company account. Store passwords in the company password manager and never reuse
a password across services.

Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required on all company accounts. Access to
production systems additionally requires a hardware security key.

## Reporting a security incident

If you suspect a security incident (a phishing email you clicked, malware, a leaked
credential or unusual account activity), report it to the security team within 1 hour.
Email security@acme-robotics.example or post in the #sec-incidents channel. Do not try to
investigate the incident yourself, and do not delete evidence.

## Laptops and devices

Company laptops must use full-disk encryption. Screens must lock automatically after
5 minutes of inactivity. Report a lost or stolen device to the IT helpdesk within 4 hours
so it can be remotely wiped.

## Data classification

All company data falls into one of four classification levels: Public, Internal,
Confidential and Restricted. Restricted data includes customer personal data and
firmware signing keys, and it must never leave approved systems.

## Access reviews and training

Managers review their team's system access every quarter. Everyone takes part in
monthly phishing simulations and completes security awareness training once a year.
